RajeevFest

A Celebration of Rajeev Alur

July 24th, 2026 · Lisbon, Portugal
Co-located with FLoC 2026

This workshop celebrates the contributions of Rajeev Alur (on the occasion of his 60th birthday) to the theory and applications of logic in computer science, computer-aided verification, and the foundations of computer science. In addition to celebrating his momentous achievements, this workshop will also honor Rajeev’s personal engagement with and service to the FLoC and broader computer science communities. We hope to see many of you there!
